Who we are

A laboratoryfor Onitsha.

CHUKS Medical Laboratory Center is a medical laboratory located in Inland Town, Onitsha, Anambra State, Nigeria.

We provide laboratory diagnostic services to patients and to the healthcare professionals who refer them. Testing gives doctors and patients information that supports better-informed decisions about health, and our role is to carry out that work with care.
