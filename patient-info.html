Before your test

A few simple things to remember.

Bring your request, if you have one

If your doctor has requested specific tests, bring that request or referral with you.

Follow your doctor’s instructions

If you’ve been given preparation instructions, follow them carefully.

Tell the team about your medication

Let the laboratory team know if you’re taking any medication or have a condition relevant to your test.

Ask if you’re unsure

It’s always fine to call or message before you travel.

Test-specific preparation. Some tests need particular preparation — fasting, for example. This varies from test to test, so please confirm with the laboratory before your visit.
