Before your test

A few simplethings to remember.

  • Bring your request, if you have oneIf your doctor has requested specific tests, bring that request or referral with you.
  • Follow your doctor’s instructionsIf you’ve been given preparation instructions, follow them carefully.
  • Tell the team about your medicationLet the laboratory team know if you’re taking any medication or have a condition relevant to your test.
  • Ask if you’re unsureIt’s always fine to call or message before you travel.

Test-specific preparation. Some tests need particular preparation — fasting, for example. This varies from test to test, so please confirm with the laboratory before your visit.
